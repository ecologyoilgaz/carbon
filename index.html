How It Works

  1. Choose a project on any of the 9 sector sites.
  2. Send USDT to the Treasury with a memo (e.g., ID: khaidarkan-mercury).
  3. Receive €ECO or €GREEN as a digital receipt.
  4. Project executes and submits report (photo + GPS).
  5. Satellite verifies the impact (Sentinel-2, TROPOMI, etc.).
  6. Token becomes "Verified" — a permanent ESG certificate.
