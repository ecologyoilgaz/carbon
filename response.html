Right of Reply

If you are named in our database and disagree with the published data, you have the right to respond. Your reply will be published alongside the original claim within 14 days.

Send your reply directly

Please include:

  • Project ID: the object you are responding to (e.g., khaidarkan-mercury)
  • Your name / organization
  • Your statement: what you disagree with and why
  • Evidence: documents, links, data that support your position
  • Contact: email or phone for verification

Send Reply to ecologyoilgaz@gmail.com

Rules for Right of Reply

  • We publish your reply alongside the original claim, without edits.
  • We do not remove the original claim unless it is proven factually wrong.
  • If your evidence changes the picture — we update the status (e.g., from 🔴 to 🟢).
  • If your reply contains threats or misinformation — we do not publish it, but we note that a reply was received.
Our commitment: We do not hide replies. Every response is published next to the claim within 14 days, even if it contradicts our data.
Note: Right of Reply is not a way to remove data. It is a way to present your side. If the data is wrong — we correct it publicly and note the correction.
